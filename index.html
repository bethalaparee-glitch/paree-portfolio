    About

    Hi, I'm Paree.

    I'm obsessed with the space where fashion meets AI. I use AI to make films that feel like real campaigns, but the idea, the taste and the edit always come from me.

    Every film starts with the brand, not the prompt. Who is it for? What does it stand for? What would make someone stop scrolling? That's the fashion business side of me, which I'm studying at Istituto Marangoni, now in Milan.

    So far: four films for the Cartier Cross-Campus Challenge, full brand strategies for Gentle Monster and Dsquared2, and freelance work for brands that find me. Always up for the next one.
